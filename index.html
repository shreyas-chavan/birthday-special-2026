Happy Birthday, Sweek. ❤️

Some stories are written in books.

Ours was written in thousands of little moments — the laughter, the ridiculous conversations, the random dinners, the birthdays, the long drives, and the late-night conversations where I would inevitably doze off somewhere in the middle.

From the two kids who met back in 2015 to everything we've become since... somehow, through all these years and all these changes, you're still one of my favourite parts of my life.

You've seen me grow, you've called me out when I needed it, you've made me laugh when I didn't feel like laughing, and you've helped me become a better version of myself without even trying to.

And now, after all these years, distance has become another chapter of our story. Different cities, different routines, and a little more distance between us — but none of that changes how much you mean to me.

I miss the ordinary things the most. The random plans. Sitting together and talking about absolutely everything. Celebrating birthdays. Just being around you without needing a reason.

But if there's one thing I've learnt from us, it's that the best memories were never really about where we were. They were about who I was with.

So here's to everything we've already written, everything we're still figuring out, and all the moments that haven't happened yet.

And somehow, we're still writing it.

More chapters to come. ✨
